07

What the data points to

MIX

Chase the $5K+ project

Larger jobs are 12% of work but half of revenue. Prioritizing panel upgrades, rewires, generators and multi-circuit installs over one-off service calls lifts both revenue and margin toward the 65% benchmark.

DEMAND

Ride storm-hardening & electrification

Pinellas standby-generator permits jumped ~50% in 2024; EV charging is growing ~28%/yr and heat-pump conversions force panel upgrades. These are natural, high-ticket adjacencies to today's service base.

DATABASE

Reactivate 1,165 dormant leads

The imported "New Lead" list is worth almost nothing today. A structured re-engagement campaign against an aging (median home built 1977–78) housing stock could surface panel and safety upgrades at near-zero acquisition cost.

GEOGRAPHY

Grow the Tampa side

83% of revenue is in Pinellas while Hillsborough adds the region's fastest population growth. A deliberate push east converts proximity into pipeline where demand is expanding quickest.
